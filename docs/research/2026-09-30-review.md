  Recheck that boundary when maintaining the catalog. Published model specs

  interpretation: the current pause notice supersedes the preserved monthly

Strict analysis does not prove provider semantics or make explicit wire maps
safe automatically. The broad exploratory Python run found substantial existing
typing debt; the initial gate makes a maintained boundary enforceable without
blanket ignores or pretending the router is fully typed. Grow that scope as
bounded implementation work. Generated locks and indexes must be maintained
through their source/generator rather than patched as independent truth.

## Roadmap implications

These findings inform the existing roadmap; ROADMAP Next governs execution order.

1. Keep Claude Keychain/config-directory discovery and idle-machine grant
   evidence at the head of provider work. Claude's optional weekly refill can
   preserve its scheduled reset; require before/after metadata and an
   authoritative reset indicator before relaxing drift admission.
2. Establish execution scope for LM Link, forwarded endpoints, and Lemonade
   composites. Improve authentication/unreachable/detail-failure diagnostics,
   loaded-instance identity, and current versus maximum context. Verify new
   Ollama list-level capabilities without losing upstream exclusions.
3. Validate harness account/model/transport and spend behavior. Caller-owned
   routers can use quotabot's admission and capacity evidence. Their task
   classifiers, embeddings, and new Ollama SystemOne decision models perform
   inference and stay outside the collector. Retries and fallback graphs need
   their own spend and lease checks.
4. Improve replay-based routing evaluation and widen strict Python coverage.
   Declare outcomes such as false-safe routes, stalls, stranded included quota,
   freshness, and lease contention. Quality, throughput, and predicted prompt
   counts require separate evidence and must not be inferred from model names.

No new adapter is justified solely by a popular model announcement. Current
llama.cpp router reads need a no-load/no-wake contract; `/props` and `/slots`
can expose content. Ollama cloud's monthly credit plans and OpenRouter's free
daily counters need typed spend/scope evidence rather than local/free labels.
